Lucky Wings — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

Privacy PolicyDelete your account

1. Who we are

Lucky Wings (the “App”) is a mobile game with a daily play goal and a monthly prize draw, published by HCR Developers (“we”, “us”). For any privacy question or request write to hcrdevelopers@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

WhatWhy
Account details — email address and display name if you create an account; your Google account name and email if you sign in with Google. Guests are identified only by a random anonymous ID.To sign you in, keep your progress, show your name on the leaderboard and contact winners.
Gameplay and raffle data — scores, best score, active play time per day, number of games, the days you qualified, and your raffle entries.To run the daily goal and monthly draw, build the leaderboard and check that entries are genuine.
Anti-cheat data — session start and end times, periodic “heartbeat” signals and a session checksum.To make sure entries are earned by real play and cannot be forged or replayed.
Notification data — a device push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and your reminders on/off choice.To send reminders and tell winners they won. You can turn reminders off in the Account screen or in your phone settings.
Advertising data — advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions, collected by Google AdMob.To show and measure ads. See section 5.
Stored on your device only — coins, fruit, unlocked birds and maps, sound and tutorial settings, and a queue of games waiting to sync.To run the game offline. This data is not sent to us, and it is removed if you delete the App or your account.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or payment card details, and the App has no in-app purchases.

3. How we use your information

  • Provide the game, your account, the leaderboard and the monthly prize draw.
  • Detect cheating and protect the fairness of the draw.
  • Contact winners and deliver prizes.
  • Send reminders and winner notifications you have allowed.
  • Show advertising that supports the App, within the choices described in section 5.
  • Keep the service secure, fix problems and meet legal obligations.

Where the law requires a legal basis (for example in the EU/UK): performance of our contract with you (account, game, draw), our legitimate interest in security and fair play, your consent (personalised ads, push notifications) and legal obligations.

4. Who sees your information

  • Other players: your display name, avatar, best score and number of raffle entries appear on the monthly leaderboard. Your email address is never shown to other players.
  • Service providers: Google LLC and its affiliates process data for us through Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging) and Google Sign-In, and show ads through Google AdMob. Their own privacy policies apply to their processing: Firebase · Google.
  • Legal: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety and the fairness of the draw.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Advertising and your choices

The App shows banner, interstitial, native, app-open and rewarded ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google’s consent form before ads are requested, and you can change your choice later.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your phone’s Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional: you only watch one if you tap the button.

More: How Google uses data in ads.

6. Prize draw

One winner is drawn each month from the raffle entries earned through play. If you win we contact you using the email address linked to your account, so guests should link an account to be reachable. We may ask for the details needed to deliver the prize and keep a record of each draw and its outcome (see section 7). The official rules of the draw are published separately by HCR Developers.

7. How long we keep information

  • Account and gameplay data: until you delete your account.
  • Records of past draws (who won which prize and when): kept for our records and legal obligations. After you delete your account this record no longer contains your name or email.
  • Technical logs and backups at our service providers are removed on their normal short schedules.

8. Your rights and deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time inside the App (Account → Delete account) or by following the steps on this page. Depending on where you live you may also have the right to access, correct, export or restrict the use of your information, to object to processing and to withdraw consent. Write to hcrdevelopers@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU/UK you may also complain to your data protection authority. California residents have the rights described in the CCPA; we do not sell or “share” personal information as those terms are defined there.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher age required in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data. Where the law requires it, a prize won by a minor is awarded through a parent or legal guardian, whose consent we may ask for. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security and international transfers

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google Cloud (Firebase) in data centres that may be outside your country; Google applies recognised transfer safeguards. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit access to personal data to what the service needs.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the App. Using the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
