Privacy PolicyDelete your account

Option 1 — delete it in the app (instant)

  1. Open Lucky Wings.
  2. On the main menu tap your name / account icon (top left).
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the Account screen and tap DELETE ACCOUNT.
  4. Confirm. If you signed up with email you will be asked for your password; if you use Google you will pick your Google account again. Guests need no extra step.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Option 2 — ask us to delete it

If you can no longer open the app, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from the address registered on your account with the subject “Delete my Lucky Wings account” and, if you know it, your display name. We confirm and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: display name, email address, avatar and push-notification token.
  • Your scores, play time, qualified days and raffle entries.
  • Your sign-in (email/password, Google link or guest ID).
  • Anything stored on your phone (coins, unlocks, queued games) is removed from the app at the same time.

What is kept

  • If you ever won a monthly draw, the record of that draw (month, prize, dates) is kept for our records and legal obligations. It no longer contains your name or email.
  • Short-lived backups and technical logs at our cloud provider are removed on their normal schedule.

Deleting your account cannot be undone. Deleting only the app from your phone does not delete your account or its data.
