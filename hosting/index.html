How it works

  1. 1

    Play every day

    Fly, dodge and beat your best score. Every full 5 minutes of play in a day earns you a raffle entry.

  2. 2

    Qualify

    Your first 5 minutes of the day qualify that day. Keep playing to make up for any day you missed.

  3. 3

    Win

    Once a month one winner is drawn. The more entries you hold, the better your chances. Winners have 7 days to claim in the app.

Age & fair play

Lucky Wings is made for teens and adults. It is suitable for players aged 13 and over (or the minimum age in your country, if higher).

Players who are under the age of majority where they live should ask a parent or guardian before joining the prize draw. Where the law requires it, a prize won by a minor is awarded through a parent or legal guardian.

We don’t knowingly collect information from children under 13. See our Privacy Policy for details.
